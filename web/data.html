Where New York takes a yellow cab

Every yellow medallion taxi trip the city publishes, attributed to the zone it started in and rolled up by day. Pick a metric, hover a zone, step through the months.

Yellow cabs are one part of the city's for-hire market — about an eighth of what New Yorkers spend on rides, and far less outside Manhattan. Read these numbers as the yellow taxi, not as the whole street.

The map

eight metrics · whole history

Every trip carries the zone it was picked up in, so the data is already a map. The outlines come from the city's taxi zone shapefile. Hover a zone to read its whole profile, not just the metric it is coloured by.

Colour is a ranking: six bands holding the same number of zones each. An average built on a handful of trips is a noisy average, so the tooltip always shows how many trips it stands on.

About this page

Source: the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission trip record data, aggregated by pickup zone and pickup day. The page is static — the whole history is a few megabytes, so it is baked into the file and nothing is fetched while you read it. Anyone who wants every row reads the Parquet instead.
